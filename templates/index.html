What this is

Search any BSE-listed company, browse its corporate announcements, and build a watchlist. New announcements for watchlisted companies are detected automatically and sent to you on Telegram.

How monitoring works

BSE doesn't push announcements to us — there's no webhook. A background process (poller.py) checks BSE for each watchlisted company on a schedule and compares against what it's already seen.

Market hours (Mon–Fri 9:00–15:30 IST): every watchlisted company is checked every 30 seconds. This is the same for every company, always — it never changes.

Outside market hours: checking every company every 30s isn't necessary, so by default each company is checked once every 15–45 minutes (randomized) to stay lightweight.

Off-hours priority polling

Sometimes you might expect a specific company to announce something at any time — including outside market hours (e.g. weekend acquisition news). Turning on off-hours priority for that company (from the Watchlist tab) checks it every 5 minutes off-hours instead of waiting up to 45 minutes. This only affects off-hours behavior — market-hours polling is unaffected either way. The Off-Hours Pollers tab lists every company currently set this way.

Alerts

When a new announcement is found, it's recorded immediately and queued for delivery to Telegram. If Telegram is briefly unreachable, delivery retries automatically on the next check — detection and delivery are decoupled, so a Telegram hiccup never causes a missed announcement.

This rides the same unofficial, undocumented JSON endpoints the BSE website itself uses — there's no official API or SLA. It can change or rate-limit without notice. Fine for personal use; don't build anything commercial or mission-critical on it.
